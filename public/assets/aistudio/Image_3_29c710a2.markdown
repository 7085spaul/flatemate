## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clean, modern, and youthful yet professional aesthetic tailored for a real estate and roommate matching platform in Bengaluru. The brand identity bridges the gap between the energetic aspirations of young professionals and students and the trustworthiness required in housing decisions. 

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with **Corporate Modern** influences: heavy reliance on generous whitespace, pristine structural cards, and a vibrant, human-centric color palette that avoids cold corporate tropes. The interface evokes a sense of clarity, reliability, and effortless social connection.

## Colors

The color palette is built on a vibrant, energetic chromatic scheme paired with clean slate neutrals. 

- **Primary (`#F900FC`):** Communicates bold energy, creativity, and modern living. Used for primary actions, active states, and key brand touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#0AF51C`):** Provides a striking, high-impact accent for highlights, verified badges, and secondary actions.
- **Neutrals:** The canvas utilizes `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for a soft, low-fatigue background, contrasted against crisp `#FFFFFF` cards and deep `#0F172A` text.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for warm, approachable headlines with **Inter** for clean, highly readable body copy. 

Ensure line heights remain generous to enhance scanning across property listings and roommate profiles. For mobile viewports, scale down any headline larger than `headline-lg` by 25% to maintain optimal proportions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **fluid grid** model optimized for high-density information display (listings, filters, and user cards). 

- **Grid:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop, scaling down to 4 columns on mobile.
- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (`< 640px`), Tablet (`640px - 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`).
- **Rhythm:** Maintain consistent vertical rhythm using multiples of the 8px base unit. Outer margins adapt from 1rem on mobile to 2.5rem on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through a hybrid approach combining **tonal layers** and **ambient shadows**. 

- **Surfaces:** The base canvas sits at the lowest plane (Slate 50). Interactive cards and navigation bars float above on crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Shadows:** Use ultra-soft, diffused shadows (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) to suggest lightweight elevation without visual clutter. Avoid harsh, high-opacity black drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs a friendly, rounded shape language (`rounded-2xl` equivalents for primary containers). 

- **Containers & Cards:** Default to `1rem` to `1.5rem` border radii, creating an inviting, modern feel.
- **Buttons & Badges:** Use pill shapes (`9999px`) for interactive triggers, filter chips, and status indicators to emphasize approachability and touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons feature the vibrant primary background with white text, pill-shaped or softly rounded corners, and a subtle scale transition on hover. Secondary buttons use ghost or outlined variants.

### Cards
Property and roommate cards are constructed on pure white surfaces with `rounded-2xl` corners, subtle ambient shadows, and internal padding of `space-lg`. Image containers occupy the top half with an aspect ratio of 16:9.

### Input Fields
Form inputs feature a neutral background, `1px` subtle borders, and `rounded-xl` corners. Focus states transition smoothly to the primary outline with a matching soft glow ring.

### Chips & Tags
Used extensively for amenity tags, budget ranges, and lifestyle preferences. Styled as small pill badges with light tinted backgrounds and medium-weight text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
Custom-styled square checkboxes and circular radios utilizing the primary color for checked states, ensuring high accessibility and visual polish.
